# Staff and Principal Preparation Plan

## Objective

Prepare for Staff, Senior Staff, Principal Engineer, and PMTS individual-contributor interviews by developing technical depth, an evidence portfolio, and leadership narratives in parallel.

## 12-Week Focus Cycle

| Weeks | Focus | Deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-2 | Baseline and architecture | Competency self-assessment; flagship-system problem statement; first design document |
| 3-4 | Data and distributed systems | Data model; event flow; idempotency, retry, and failure-handling design |
| 5-6 | Implementation and quality | Working vertical slice; tests; code-review checklist; ADRs |
| 7-8 | Reliability and operations | SLOs, dashboards, load tests, capacity assumptions, and a failure drill |
| 9-10 | Leadership and strategy | Migration roadmap; stakeholder map; ten measurable leadership stories |
| 11-12 | Interview simulation | Coding, system-design, behavioral, and project deep-dive mocks |

## Weekly Cadence

- Two DSA/coding sessions, emphasizing communication and complexity.
- Two system-design sessions, emphasizing requirements, trade-offs, and failure handling.
- One implementation or operational-improvement session on the flagship project.
- One leadership-story or behavioral mock.
- One review of a design document, ADR, incident report, or architecture standard.

## Flagship Project Standard

The project should demonstrate multi-tenancy, APIs, a relational data store, cache, asynchronous events, authentication/authorization, observability, deployment automation, and tested failure handling. It must have documentation that lets another engineer understand the system without verbal context.

## Interview Readiness Criteria

You are ready to apply when you can:

- Complete coding problems with a correct, well-explained solution and complexity analysis.
- Lead a 45- to 60-minute system-design discussion from ambiguous requirements to a justified architecture.
- Explain one project from business problem through launch, operations, trade-offs, and measured result.
- Give evidence-based stories of influence, conflict resolution, mentorship, quality improvement, and customer impact.
- Explain your technical vision as a staged plan with risks, dependencies, milestones, and success metrics.
